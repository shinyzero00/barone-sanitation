# Barone Sanitation

Local waste-service storefront and dispatch desk. Practical, established, restrained; the visual reference is printed office stationery. The signature device is a ruled receipt or ledger, not floating dashboard cards.

Cream paper, deep green masthead, charcoal text, burgundy alerts. Georgia for editorial headings, PT Sans where available for working text. Type starts at 1rem and grows approximately by 1.25; space follows a 4px unit. Use sharp edges, rules instead of shadows and locally replaceable illustrations. Keep controls labeled and focus visible. No dark theme or decorative motion.
